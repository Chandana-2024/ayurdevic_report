# Safety and Disclaimers

All Ayurveda material is traditional wellness education, not diagnosis, treatment, prevention, a cure, or a guaranteed outcome. Disease screening is research/screening only and never confirmation. Seek qualified clinical care for severe, urgent, worsening, pregnancy-related, medication-related, or condition-related concerns.

The final report must state that it is educational and that doctor review/approval is required for AI material. Medicine and prescription advice appears only when entered or explicitly approved by the doctor.
